THE ELVORA EXPERIENCE · DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

One platform.
More ways to explore.

ELVORA STREAMING is being planned as a hybrid entertainment companion: built-in discovery, an optional add-on ecosystem, and tools that make your personal library feel like home.

Planned features — currently in development

These are product goals, not a list of features already available in a released app. Features may change as development and testing progress.

⌕

Built-in Discovery

Explore movies, series, and anime through organized categories, search, details, and discovery feeds.

01 / DISCOVER
⌘

Verified Add-on Center

Find, install, manage, and update optional add-ons from sources that meet the app's safety and policy checks.

02 / EXTEND
▤

Personal Library

Keep a watchlist, favorites, saved collections, and a Continue Watching shelf in one place.

03 / ORGANIZE
▶

Advanced Player

Plan for subtitle and audio-track selection, playback speed, and useful playback controls.

04 / PLAY
✧

Smart Recommendations

Discover titles based on selected interests and activity, with clear controls over personalization.

05 / PERSONALIZE
◎

Profiles & Parental Controls

Separate profiles and age-appropriate controls are planned for a more flexible shared experience.

06 / PROFILES
◉

Watch Party

A future social feature for synchronized viewing with friends, subject to technical and content-source support.

07 / TOGETHER
↻

Updates & Improvements

Release notes, version information, bug fixes, and new capabilities from the official project hub.

08 / EVOLVE

ELVORA STREAMING will support only content sources that users are authorized to access. Add-ons may be independently operated; availability, safety checks, and compatibility will be explained before use. No movie or anime catalogue is promised until its lawful source and access rights are confirmed.

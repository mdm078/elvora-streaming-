Privacy Policy.

Important: This policy describes the current public website and early browser-based app preview. It is not a statement about a future Android release. We will review and update it before introducing accounts, online profiles, analytics, advertising, payments, streaming integrations, or other data-processing features.

1. Who operates this project

ELVORA STREAMING is an independent project. This website is currently published using GitHub Pages. GitHub provides the hosting infrastructure and may process technical request information under its own policies. We do not control GitHub's infrastructure logs. Review GitHub's privacy statement for details about its processing.

2. Information used by the current preview

  • Theme preference: The website stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage so it can remember the setting on that device.
  • App preview preferences: The browser-based app prototype stores its sample watchlist and theme preference in local storage on your device. The prototype does not currently provide user accounts or cloud synchronization.
  • Basic technical requests: When you load the site, your browser makes requests to the hosting provider. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request time as part of hosting and security operations.
  • Fonts: The current design loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google to retrieve those font files; Google's own privacy terms apply to that request.

3. Information we do not currently ask you to provide

The current website and prototype do not offer account registration, payment, subscription, direct messaging, or a cloud profile. They do not ask you to submit your real name, phone number, precise location, or payment details. Do not enter sensitive information into this preview.

4. Add-ons and external services

The Add-on Center is currently an informational placeholder. No add-on installation or third-party streaming source is enabled in this preview. If external providers are integrated in a future version, we will explain what information is shared, why it is needed, and where their separate privacy terms can be found before launch.

5. Retention and deletion

Theme and sample watchlist data are stored in your browser's local storage. You can clear this data using your browser's site-data controls. Removing local data may reset your saved theme and sample list. Hosting-provider logs are handled according to the provider's retention practices and policies.

6. Children

The current preview is not designed to collect personal information from children. Any future child or family features will require a separate review and appropriate safeguards before release.

7. Security and changes

We take reasonable care when developing the project, but a prototype is not a guarantee of uninterrupted or error-free service. This policy will change as the product evolves. The date above indicates the latest revision of this page.

8. Contact

A dedicated support email has not yet been set up. Until an official contact address is published here, please do not send personal or sensitive information about yourself through unofficial channels.

Pre-launch notice: This document is a project-stage privacy explanation, not legal advice or a final policy for a released Android app. It must be reviewed against the actual hosting configuration, app code, SDKs, and applicable law before public app distribution.
